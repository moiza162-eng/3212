Why Google Search clicks fall when impressions rise

Check what changed in the mix

A page can appear more often while getting fewer clicks if it starts showing for broader queries. Compare equivalent periods in Search Console and examine queries and pages separately. Look at branded and non-branded terms, then check device and country where you have enough data. Avoid reading too much into a small sample or a holiday week.

Diagnose the affected pages

Suppose course pages are steady but a new guide contributes many impressions and few clicks. That differs from a decline on an established registration page. For the latter, check whether its position changed for the queries that used to drive traffic. Review what the search result promises and whether the page answers that need.

Test one change at a time

If a result looks vague for an important query, revise the title so it states the offer and the intended audience. Keep a note of the date and compare click-through rate, clicks and impressions once there is enough comparable data. Check enquiry volume separately. Search Console describes search visibility, while your site analytics can help explain what visitors did after arriving.
