Social media

Build a social content calendar from customer questions

Collect the questions you already hear

Review enquiries, comments and common questions from colleagues. Group them into recurring decisions such as cost, eligibility and what happens after signup. Use real patterns without exposing private messages or personal information. Choose three topics that help people take the next step.

Turn each question into a useful post

A question about classroom attendance could become a short video showing the venue and explaining which sessions are online. A question about eligibility might work better as a carousel with an example and a link to the full criteria. Put the answer up front, add captions to video and give the post a clear destination when people need more detail.

Review after a manageable trial

Try a two-week calendar and track the topic, format, intended action and result. Compare saves for practical guides, qualified clicks for registration posts and recurring comments for missing information. Use those signals to plan the next set of posts. Keep an idea bank so seasonal announcements do not crowd out the questions people ask year-round.
