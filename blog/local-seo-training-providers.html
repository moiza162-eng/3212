Local SEO

Local SEO for training providers

Make the location part of the decision

A learner deciding whether to attend in person needs more than a city name. Put the teaching venue, delivery format and practical travel details on the course page. If enrolment takes place online but classes take place in Islington, state both clearly beside the registration link.

Review the public listings

Compare the website's contact information with the organisation's Google Business Profile, where eligible. Confirm the address, phone number, opening hours and destination URL with the team that manages them. If classes use a separate venue or follow different hours, explain the distinction on the site. Ask a colleague unfamiliar with the venue to find directions and report what was unclear.

Measure the actions that matter

Keep a record of local search themes and the pages that receive clicks for them. Track actions such as calls, directions clicks or completed enquiries where your tools support them. A change in direction requests might reflect a new cohort or an event, so annotate dates before drawing conclusions. Review results alongside the quality of enquiries and the questions the front desk receives.
